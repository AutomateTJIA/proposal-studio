# What to pull from each quote

Read every page (`pdftotext -layout <file>`). Copy amounts exactly as printed.

## Every quote
Client names and mailing address · carrier and underwriting company · quote number · date prepared ·
policy period / effective date · 12-month premium · paid-in-full price and discount · every payment
plan (down payment, installment amount, number of installments, fees, total) · discounts listed ·
NC rate-deviation notice (approved rate vs quoted premium), if present.

## Auto
Each vehicle: year, make, model, trim, VIN, use, garaging ZIP · each driver: name, DOB/age, status
(rated/excluded), marital status · each coverage line per vehicle: limit, deductible, premium ·
policy-level coverages (UM/UIM, UMPD) and charges (pay-plan setup) · accident forgiveness /
enhanced packages · accidents and violations listed · "Accident free" / "Violation free" discounts.

## Home / landlord
Property address · Coverage A–F limits · extended/additional dwelling % · deductibles (all-peril,
wind/hail) · roof settlement (actual cash value endorsements) · endorsements with limits and
premiums (water back-up, service line / buried utility, loss assessment, replacement cost) ·
property details (year built, sq ft, stories, construction, foundation, garage, roof material /
age / area, heating, A/C, protective devices) · reconstruction cost estimate.

## Disability income
Insured age, sex, nicotine, occupation class, state, income · monthly benefit and % of income ·
waiting (elimination) period · benefit period · base premium and every rider premium · discounts
(and what they require) · modal premiums (annual, semi-annual, quarterly, monthly) and installment
charges · noncancelable / guaranteed renewable terms · definition of disability · partial/residual
thresholds · mental-disorder and substance limitations · pre-existing condition terms · survivor /
death benefits · increase riders · alternate waiting/benefit-period premium tables · underwriting
requirements (exam, labs, income proof) · dividends (participating policies).
- **The Standard (Platinum Advantage):** page 2 summary with riders; page 3 alternate-period grid;
  noncancelable is an optional rider on a guaranteed-renewable base; eApply discount.
- **Principal (Income Protector):** page 2 policy summary; last page alternate EP/BP premiums;
  noncancelable built in; monthly mode has no extra charge.
- **MassMutual (Radius Choice):** illustration summary page 2; alternate WP/BP grid (rounded);
  24-month mental-disorder limit; participating (dividends not guaranteed); installment APR table.

## Exclusions & limitations (all lines)
- Disability: the "Exclusions", "Not covered", "Limitations" and "Pre-existing condition" sections
  (The Standard: "Coverage Exclusions and Limitations" near the end; Principal: "Exclusions and
  limitations" summary plus "Limitations when outside the United States or Canada", "Limitations due to
  normal pregnancy or childbirth", "Loss of license"; MassMutual: "NOT COVERED", "MENTAL DISORDER
  LIMITATION", "LIMITATION OF PRE-EXISTING CONDITIONS", reduced benefit periods by age). Also mental
  health / substance limits, military suspension, living-abroad limits, renewal-after-65 terms.
- Home: roof settlement (actual cash value vs replacement cost), separate wind/hail deductibles, water
  back-up limit, service line / buried utility lines (present? limit?), loss assessment, liability limit;
  flood and earthquake are never in a standard home quote unless listed as an endorsement.
- Auto: low property damage / UMPD limits, rental caps, anything excluded or limited on the quote.

## Common carrier layouts
- **Liberty Mutual (Safeco home / American States auto):** summary page with coverages and
  payment-plan table; second page lists EFT / RCC / mail options; dwelling details page has the
  reconstruction estimate. Auto shows "Enhanced" packages and accident forgiveness.
- **Travelers:** home and auto often in one PDF; separate monthly-billing table; auto shows an
  "Accidents, Violations, and Losses" section.
- **National General (Encompass home / Integon auto):** auto charges UM/UIM once on vehicle 1 and
  adds a $40 pay-plan setup charge; home lists wind/hail deductible and ACV roof endorsements.
