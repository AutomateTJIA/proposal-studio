New proposal

One client per proposal. Put each carrier's quotes in its own slot. If a carrier sent separate home and auto quotes, drop both into the same slot.

Tell the proposal writer what matters: what to mention, which carrier to favor and why, dates, what the client cares about. It follows these in the PDF and the email. Prices and coverages always come straight from the quotes.

When it's done, the PDF and a ready-to-copy client email are emailed to Billy and Chelssy. Nothing is sent to the client.
